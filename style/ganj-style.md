# Style reference: "3D map documentary" (vertical, 9:16)

Reference: a 127 s TikTok clip, about 17 hard cuts, roughly 1 shot every 5–8 s. Only the style is used here; nothing is copied.

## Look
- Photoreal 3D relief map of the country:
  - satellite texture, snow on the peaks, clouds, a slow drone-style camera (tilt and push).
  - The camera dives from space down to the province.
- Night-to-dusk palette: deep navy and black, warm gold light, blue cold glow for "data" layers.
- Points of interest:
  - a glowing orb or light pillar on the map, with a ring pulse;
  - a 3D object rises out of the ground (gem, ore block, crystal, phone, battery).
- Studio "museum" scenes: dark grey cyclorama and cylinders or pedestals holding a hero object, lit by a soft top spot.
- Comparison scenes: country slabs on round pedestals with gold coin stacks or light beams for the numbers, or a scale field (for example, Eiffel towers repeated to the horizon).
- Night-lights-from-space map with a glowing gold border line; routes (rail, roads) draw on as lines.

## Typography and UI
- A serif title per chapter (place name) with a small italic subtitle (the resource) and a coloured dot.
- A top tracker: "treasures 04/10" with coloured progress dots.
- Small caps kicker labels, for example "• IN YOUR PHONE · SPEAKER" or "✓ RAILWAY TO THE SEA", forming a checklist that builds line by line.
- Big numbers:
  - a rolling odometer counter (slot digits) with a tiny caption under it;
  - flip-clock year cards (1976, 1980, 2026).
- A paper note card with a typewriter cursor for quotes or memos.
- Rhetorical question in serif, with a coloured italic keyword ("still poor?").

## Captions (the signature)
- One or two words at a time, bold white sans, lower-middle of the frame, synced to the voice.
- The keyword in each phrase is coloured:
  - gold for wealth;
  - red for war or poverty;
  - cyan or blue for places;
  - purple or green for the resource colour.

## Structure (about 2 min)
1. Hook:
   - Title over a split frame: a village above ground, glowing veins below ground.
   - A huge counter: "trillion".
2. Human shot: a single person walking in a poor village.
3. "How do we know?": satellite or plane scan, data glow.
4. Chapters 01 to N, one per place:
   - map dive;
   - resource object;
   - a real-life tie-in (your phone, your cable);
   - number.
5. Turn: "So why are we still poor?" → a checklist of what is missing; years of war counter.
6. Counterfactual: back to 1978; routes and factories light up the map; comparison with a peer country.
7. Engagement question ("yes or no?"), loop back to the opening image, then a handle card.

## Sound
Dark ambient bed, soft risers into each chapter, whooshes on camera dives, ticks on counters, deep hits on the big reveals.

## How we build it here
- Remotion: map plates, overlays, counters, captions and the tracker.
- Blender or Higgsfield images and clips: 3D relief, objects, studio pedestals.
- Our own voice and script. Kashida and Quds rules apply to Pashto text.
