# Standing rules for videos in this repo (from the owner)

- Reply to the owner in Pashto.
- Never put figures, counts or details on screen that are not in the provided script/content.
- Render every film in ONE continuous pass (no stitched parts); deliver a HandBrake copy compressed without visible quality loss.
- Send live screenshots while working and the final file at the end.
- Higgsfield: don't waste credits; stay within the budget the owner gives.
- Type: Al-Quds only (heavy weights for titles; thinned Bold elsewhere). Justify with kashida where needed.
- Every scene must always have moving subjects — never a still screen. Fill the frame; avoid big empty areas.
- Hindukush films END with the Hindukush end card: three discs (#EAA500, white, #051449) grow in turn and cover
  the frame, the light logo glitches in on navy, "@hindukush" fades in below (see hindukush/web/src/scenes.js).
- Sound effects: use ready-made, professional libraries (Mixkit, Higgsfield sound effects, …) — never
  improvised synth beeps or toy-like/comic sounds. Every object entrance and action gets a fitting, dignified
  sound (transitions, typing, semantic hits); keep background music low under the narration.
- Hindukush explainers carry a narration caption bar above the logo at the bottom: a primary-colour panel where
  each sentence is written as it is read, with a speaking/voice icon on the right, with smooth transitions.
- Backgrounds: no plain floating circles; use soft, low-opacity primary-colour grids with invisible edges.
- Always speak to the owner in Pashto, including every progress update (see rules.md).
